3. Responsible-Use Principles (Non-Binding)

While the IMESI Works are freely available for any use, the author encourages (but does not legally require) future users to observe the following non-binding responsible-use principles:

1. Defensive Purpose - The IMESI framework was designed primarily as a defensive mechanism for collective economic resilience and sovereign stability, not as an offensive or coercive instrument.

2. Proportionality - Any activation or use of mechanisms derived from the IMESI Works should be proportionate to the economic pressure or coercion being addressed.

3. Transparency - Where feasible, users should communicate their intent and rationale when invoking or adapting IMESI-related mechanisms, to reduce misunderstanding and miscalculation.

4. Coalition Coordination - The framework is designed to strengthen collective action; users are encouraged to coordinate with affected partners and allies to ensure burden-sharing and mutual support.

5. Non-Discrimination - The mechanisms should not be used to unjustly target, isolate, or penalize states or actors based on factors unrelated to the qualifying economic coercion or disruption.

6. Good-Faith Interpretation - Users should interpret and implement the framework in good faith, consistent with the spirit of economic security, international cooperation, and de-escalation.
